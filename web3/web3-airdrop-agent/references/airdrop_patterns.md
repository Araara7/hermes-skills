# Airdrop Patterns & Strategies

## Common Airdrop Criteria

### Volume-Based
- Total volume traded
- Number of transactions
- Unique days active

### Duration-Based
- Days since first transaction
- Consecutive days active
- Monthly activity streak

### Multi-Chain
- Activity on multiple chains
- Bridge usage
- Cross-chain transactions

### Governance
- Token voting participation
- DAO proposal creation
- Delegation activity

## Red Flags (Scam Detection)

1. **Asking for private key** - ALWAYS scam
2. **Send ETH to receive more** - ALWAYS scam
3. **Urgency pressure** - "Claim in 24h or lose it"
4. **Unverified contracts** - Check on Etherscan/Blockscout
5. **Fake official links** - Verify domain carefully

## Gas Optimization

1. **Batch transactions** - Combine multiple claims
2. **Off-peak hours** - Lower gas on weekends/nights
3. **L2 alternatives** - Use Base/Arbitrum when possible
4. **Gas tokens** - Consider gas refund protocols

## Multi-Wallet Strategy

### Pros
- Increase allocation
- Different criteria per wallet
- Risk distribution

### Cons
- Sybil detection risk
- Higher management overhead
- Gas costs multiply

### Best Practices
- Different funding sources
- Varied activity patterns
- No obvious connections
- Separate IPs/devices ideal

## Tracking Tools

- **DeBank** - Portfolio tracking
- **Zapper** - Multi-chain dashboard
- **Arkham** - Wallet labeling
- **Dune Analytics** - Custom queries

## Claim Timing

1. **Early claim** - Confirm allocation, pay higher gas
2. **Wait for gas** - Save on fees, risk missing window
3. **Monitor** - Check claim contract status first
